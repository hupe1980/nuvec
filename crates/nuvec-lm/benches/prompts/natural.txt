The storm had been building over the bay since noon, and by the time Mara reached the harbour the fishing boats were already straining at their ropes. She pulled her coat tighter and walked past the shuttered café, the chandlery with its window of brass lamps, and the old customs house where her grandfather had worked for thirty years. At the end of the pier the lighthouse stood dark against the clouds. It had not been lit in a decade, not since the new beacon was built on the headland, but tonight she had a key in her pocket and a reason to climb the stairs.
